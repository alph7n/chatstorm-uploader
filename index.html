Look Again Shorts Publisher

Look Again Shorts Publisher is a private upload tool for the Chat Storm YouTube channel (formerly Look Again). It publishes the channel's own short videos to the channel's own YouTube account through the YouTube Data API.

The tool is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's videos together with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails.
  • Reads the channel's own name and the view, like and comment counts of its own videos.
